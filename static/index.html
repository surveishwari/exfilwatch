How the gateway works

1

Integrate once

OpenAI-compatible base_url, or a single API call from your existing backend. No SDK to learn, no change to how your app already talks to its model.

2

Inspect automatically

Every outbound LLM response passes through ExfilWatch before it reaches a user — nothing is manually reviewed to get this coverage.

3

Detect covert exfiltration

Hidden Unicode channels (including the technique behind GlassWorm), zero-click image-URL leaks, exposed secrets/PII, and unnamed/unpublished schemes via category-based analysis.

4

Enforce policy

ALLOW, REVIEW, or BLOCK — and on BLOCK, the underlying clean reply is independently re-scanned and served automatically, or escalated. A decision, not just an alert.

5

Investigate & audit

Security teams see every incident, its evidence and risk derivation, and a tamper-evident hash-chained audit trail — on the Dashboard, after the fact, not in the request path.

Test & Investigate secondary tools

The Live Console and Scanner below are for trying a payload yourself or investigating a specific case — not the production workflow. In production, step 2 above runs on every response with nobody watching a screen.

Live attack console

Generate a real model reply, inject a covert payload, and watch it get decoded — useful for demos and hypothesis testing, not for routing real traffic.
Investigation dashboard

Review incidents the gateway already caught automatically — audit trail, evidence, live validation and self red-team results.
